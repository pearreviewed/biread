The finest reading matches your books by meaning — so you bring the model.

Two translations share meaning, not words, so lining them up takes a model rather than a trick. You bring your own key: it stays in your browser, goes straight to OpenRouter, and never reaches us. The tool is free — you pay only the model, and you see the exact price before anything runs.

No key yet? One key covers every model — openrouter.ai/keys.

Runs on your own machine — free and private, nothing leaves your computer, no key. One-time setup: start Ollama so it lets this page in, then pull the models.
